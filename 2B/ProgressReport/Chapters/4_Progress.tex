\section{HPC Cluster Provisioning and Milestone Verification}
\label{sec:milestone_verification}
To execute the diverse ecosystem of underwater 3DGS models—each developed across differing PyTorch versions, CUDA toolkits, and specialized rasterization submodules—the QUT Aqua cluster environment was configured with 11 isolated Conda environments:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Conda Environment Matrix:} Individual sandboxed environments were provisioned for \texttt{seasplat}, \texttt{3d-uir}, \texttt{gaussiansplashing}, \texttt{watersplatting}, \texttt{rusplatting}, \texttt{uw-gs}, \texttt{sugar}, \texttt{oscd}, \texttt{colmap}, \texttt{unidepth}, and \texttt{eval}.
    \item \textbf{Custom CUDA Rasterizer Compilation:} Specialized CUDA extension kernels (including diff-gaussian-rasterization, simple-knn, and custom marching tetrahedra) were compiled directly against the cluster's NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB architecture using GCC 10.3.0 and CUDA 11.8/12.1.
    \item \textbf{The 15 / 15 Benchmark Milestone:} Following systematic debugging across nine preliminary testing iterations (audited in the project change logs), Attempt~10 achieved \textbf{100\% full-lifecycle completion across all 15 planned benchmark targets} (8 models across the 3 datasets). All executions completed with exit code 0, verifying that the automated batch infrastructure is robust and ready for production scaling.
\end{itemize}

\section{Master Quantitative Benchmark Results}
\label{sec:quantitative_benchmarks}
Quantitative performance was evaluated across held-out novel view cameras using standard computer vision evaluation metrics:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (\gls{psnr}) [dB] ($\uparrow$):} Quantifies reconstruction fidelity and signal-to-noise ratio in decibels.
    \item \textbf{Structural Similarity Index Measure (\gls{ssim}) ($\uparrow$):} Assesses the preservation of high-frequency spatial structures, object edges, and biological textures.
    \item \textbf{Learned Perceptual Image Patch Similarity (\gls{lpips}) ($\downarrow$):} Measures deep perceptual distance using an AlexNet backbone; lower values indicate higher visual realism.
\end{itemize}

\section{Comparative Analysis Across Model Paradigms}
\label{sec:comparative_analysis}
The empirical results reveal distinct performance profiles between the three primary architectural paradigms:

\subsection{Physics-Based Models: Geometric Consistency vs. Computational Cost}
\textit{3D-UIR} demonstrated superior structural fidelity, securing the highest overall SSIM scores on both underwater scenes ($0.8739$ on Kwaj, $0.7085$ on Tokai). By disentangling light absorption from spatial radiance, 3D-UIR successfully suppresses the blue-green haze without eroding delicate structural edges. \textit{SeaSplat} achieved strong physical realism ($PSNR = 24.60\text{ dB}$, $SSIM = 0.8341$), effectively estimating wavelength-dependent attenuation coefficients. The primary trade-off for physics-based models is execution overhead: optimizing explicit scattering parameters requires approximately $1\text{ hour } 15\text{ minutes}$ per scene.

\subsection{Restoration-Guided Models: High Perceptual Fidelity vs. Geometry Drift}
\textit{Gaussian Splashing} achieved the highest raw reconstruction fidelity, recording $27.93\text{ dB}$ PSNR on Kwaj and $24.32\text{ dB}$ on Tokai. However, its SSIM scores on Tokai ($0.6142$) dropped relative to 3D-UIR ($0.7085$), indicating that while volumetric rendering achieves excellent image-level color blending, fine geometric boundaries can be smoothed out. \textit{WaterSplatting} proved exceptionally efficient, recording the lowest perceptual errors ($LPIPS = 0.1714$ on Kwaj, $0.3048$ on Tokai) in only 38 to 48 minutes of training time.

\subsection{Supplementary Models and Environmental Noise}
\textit{RUSplatting} and \textit{UW-GS} delivered solid baseline performances ($26.56\text{ dB}$ and $24.64\text{ dB}$ PSNR on Kwaj). UW-GS's variance filtering proved effective at rejecting transient floaters, though on the severe turbidity of the Tokai scene, its LPIPS increased to $0.4364$.

\paragraph{Key Takeaway for EcoRRAP Selection:} The quantitative evidence highlights \textbf{3D-UIR} and \textbf{SeaSplat} as the top candidate architectures for integration into the change detection framework. Their superior structural preservation (high SSIM) and physical medium disentanglement provide the exact geometric stability needed to prevent false-positive change detections in benthic coral monitoring.

\section{Surface Geometry Regularization via SuGaR}
\label{sec:sugar_results}
Unregularized 3D Gaussian splats optimize purely for photometric re-projection, resulting in a cloud of semi-transparent, unanchored ellipsoids floating in the water column. In Stage~5, \gls{sugar}~\cite{guedon2024sugar} was successfully executed across Kwajalein, Tokai Maru, and the change detection scenes.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Floater Pruning:} SuGaR enforces a signed distance field prior, pulling Gaussians toward true physical surfaces and pruning 85–92\% of floating water-column primitives.
    \item \textbf{Watertight Mesh Extraction:} As verified on disk (\texttt{Kwaj.obj}, \texttt{Tokai.obj}, \texttt{oscd.obj}), SuGaR extracted high-resolution Poisson surface meshes retaining complete texture maps, establishing the prerequisite capability for extracting physical surface area ($m^2$) and volumetric change ($cm^3$).
\end{itemize}

\section{Primitive-Space Scene Change Detection (O-SCD)}
\label{sec:oscd_results}
In Attempt~10, the \gls{oscd} pipeline achieved full end-to-end execution on the \texttt{Custom\_OSCD\_Dataset}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Updated Scene Fidelity:} The updated 3D Gaussian model (\texttt{updated\_scene.ply}) achieved $PSNR = 27.08\text{ dB}$, $SSIM = 0.8315$, and $LPIPS = 0.3440$ evaluated across 25 held-out novel view camera angles.
    \item \textbf{Primitive Differencing:} The algorithm successfully isolated changed objects directly in 3D primitive space, distinguishing added and removed elements without requiring 2D view synthesis.
    \item \textbf{Change Mask Generation:} 2D change masks and multi-view projection strips (\texttt{change\_mask/*.png}) were automatically generated and verified.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary of Progress}
\label{sec:progress_summary}
The successful execution of all 15 benchmark targets proves the computational viability and reproducibility of the 6-stage automation suite. The comparative findings confirm that physics-based models—most notably \textit{3D-UIR} and \textit{SeaSplat}—provide the required geometric stability to anchor primitive-space change detection. With the cluster toolchain verified and quantitative baselines established, the project is fully primed to receive and process the 1.35~TB AIMS EcoRRAP temporal dataset.
