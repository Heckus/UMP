# Open Source 3D Gaussian Splatting Models for Underwater Scenes

Based on the literature review in the QUT Project Proposal and the bibliography, here are the models and approaches that have their full code officially open-sourced on GitHub.

## Scene Change Detection
- **GS-DIFF** (Galappaththige et al., 2026): [https://github.com/Chumsy0725/GS-DIFF](https://github.com/Chumsy0725/GS-DIFF)
- **3DGS-CD** (Lu et al., 2025): [https://github.com/520xyxyzq/3DGS-CD](https://github.com/520xyxyzq/3DGS-CD)

## Physically-Grounded Underwater 3DGS
- **SeaSplat** (Yang et al., 2025): [https://seasplat.github.io/](https://seasplat.github.io/)
- **RecGS** (Zhang et al., 2024): [https://github.com/tyz1030/recgs](https://github.com/tyz1030/recgs)
- **3D-UIR**: [https://bilityniu.github.io/3D-UIR/](https://bilityniu.github.io/3D-UIR/)
## Image Restoration & Prior-Driven
- **WaterSplatting** (Li et al., 2025): [https://github.com/water-splatting/water-splatting](https://github.com/water-splatting/water-splatting)
- **Gaussian Splashing**: [https://github.com/BGU-CS-VIL/gaussianSplashing](https://github.com/BGU-CS-VIL/gaussianSplashing)
## Extreme Environments & Adaptations
- **RUSplatting** (Jiang et al., 2025): [https://github.com/theflash987/RUSplatting](https://github.com/theflash987/RUSplatting)
- **UW-GS** (Wang et al., 2025): [https://github.com/WangHaoran16/UW-GS](https://github.com/WangHaoran16/UW-GS)
- **Spatiotemporal 3DGS** (Liu et al., 2025): [https://github.com/deng-ai-lab/MarineSTD-GS](https://github.com/deng-ai-lab/MarineSTD-GS)
- **Underwater360** (Hu et al., 2026): [https://github.com/SwcK423/Underwater360](https://github.com/SwcK423/Underwater360)
- **SuGaR** (Guédon & Lepetit, 2024): [https://github.com/Anttwo/SuGaR](https://github.com/Anttwo/SuGaR)

## Multi-Modal Sonar Fusion
- **SonarSplat** (Sethuraman et al., 2025): [https://github.com/umfieldrobotics/sonarsplat3D](https://github.com/umfieldrobotics/sonarsplat3D)
- **NAS-GS** :[https://senseroboticslab.github.io/NAS-GS-Page/?utm_source=catalyzex.com](https://senseroboticslab.github.io/NAS-GS-Page/?utm_source=catalyzex.com)

---
**Note:** The following methods were mentioned in the literature review but currently do not appear to have their full code officially open-sourced or easily accessible on GitHub: 
*WaterGS, DualPhys-GS, UW-3DGS, R-Splatting, Water-Adapted 3DGS, Aqua-Splat,WaterClear-GS**,Aquatic-GS**, and SonarReg-GS.*
