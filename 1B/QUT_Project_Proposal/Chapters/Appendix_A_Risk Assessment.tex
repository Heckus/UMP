\chapter{Appendix A: Risk Assessment}

The following document is the official QUT risk assessment for this project, covering laboratory work, field work, and data handling procedures. The assessment addresses specific risks related to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Waterproofing Failure:} Handled through iterative pressure testing.
    \item \textbf{Electrical Safety:} Ensure all electronics are properly insulated and handled according to standard laboratory safety procedures.
    \item \textbf{Fieldwork Hazards:} Addressed via standard operating procedures provided by QUT and AIMS.
\end{itemize}
